Try AgentDiff in your browser

Two agents ran the same task, and one went wrong. Pick a pair of runs, or drop in two traces of your own, and AgentDiff names the step where they parted, with the evidence for it. The engine runs here, in your browser, as Python compiled to WebAssembly. Nothing you choose is uploaded.
